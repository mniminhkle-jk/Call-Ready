# Pirelli Tire North America
Manufacturer · Rome, GA · BIT: PIRC

Premium car and motorcycle tire maker. US headquarters, MIRS robotized plant and R&D center in Rome, GA; plant in Silao, Mexico; sales offices in New York, Los Angeles, Detroit, Montreal and Atlanta.

**Address:** 100 Pirelli Drive, Rome, GA 30161
**Phones:** Pirelli Tire LLC, Rome GA (main): 706-368-5800 · Pirelli customer service: 1-800-747-3554

## What we found
- **Expansion:** Board approved ~€1B (~$1.2B) on Sept 22, 2026 to grow Rome to ~6M car tires a year by 2033; capacity rises from 2028. [1]
- **Jobs:** ~1,000 new jobs, mostly hired in 2029; Rome employs 234 today. [2]
- **New product:** Cyber Tyre (sensor-equipped tires) production started at Rome (announced May 2026). [3]
- **US supply (2025):** About 5% from Rome, 55% from Silao (described as 'fully saturated'), 40% from Europe and Brazil. [4]
- **Tariffs:** EU car tires 15% from Aug 2025; Brazil +25%; Mexico duty-free as USMCA-compliant; Pirelli 'revised logistics flows.' [5]
- **Ownership:** Sinochem cut its stake from 34.1% to 20.1% in July 2026. [9]

## Freight facts
- **Ships:** Finished car, SUV, motorcycle and cycling tires to OEM plants and replacement dealers. [8]
- **Warehousing:** Kuehne+Nagel runs US warehouses in McDonough, GA and Rialto, CA (800,000 sq ft), consolidating domestic shipments. [6]
- **Systems:** Moving to third-generation WMS/TMS with barcode scanning and GPS tracking (June 2025). [7]
- **Trade:** USMCA wasn't renewed in its current form on July 1, 2026; annual reviews now apply. [10]

## Locations
- Rome, GA: US HQ, MIRS plant, R&D center. [8]
- Silao, Guanajuato, Mexico: car tire plant. [8]
- Sales offices: New York, Los Angeles, Detroit, Montreal, Atlanta. [8]
