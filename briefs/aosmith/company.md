# A. O. Smith
Manufacturer · Milwaukee, WI · NYSE: AOS

Largest maker of water heaters in North America, plus boilers (Lochinvar), water treatment and, since Jan 2026, Leonard Valve. North America is about 78% of sales.

**Address:** 11270 West Park Place, Suite 170, Milwaukee, WI 53224
**Phones:** A. O. Smith Water Products, Ashland City TN (division HQ): 866-362-9898 · A. O. Smith world HQ, Milwaukee: 414-359-4000

## What we found
- **Revenue:** Q2 2026 sales about $1B; North America $820.5M, up 5%; boilers up 21%; residential water heater volumes down. [2]
- **Costs:** Steel up 20% YoY in Q2; 4–7% water heater price increases from mid-Q3, with customer pre-buy in Q2. [3]
- **Footprint:** 23 manufacturing and assembly plants in North America across 13 states and 2 other countries. [1]
- **Channels:** About 800 independent plumbing wholesalers, plus an exclusive Lowe's relationship. [1]
- **Acquisition:** Leonard Valve (Cranston, RI) acquired for $470M in Jan 2026; about $70M of 2026 sales expected. [5]
- **Disruption:** Storm damage at the Ashland City, TN plant constrained production and shipping in Q1 2026. [6]

## Freight facts
- **Ships:** Residential and commercial water heaters, boilers, pressure tanks, water-treatment systems. [1]
- **Network change:** Groveport, OH plant and Lockbourne warehouse close by Dec 11, 2026; work moves to Appleton, WI and Haltom City, TX. [4]
- **Traffic dept.:** Johnson City shipping role (Oct 2026) coordinates carriers, daily LTL pickups and DOT bills of lading. [9]
- **International:** Freight bid to carriers, forwarders and brokers; USMCA origin work (Aug 2026 posting). [10]

## Locations
- Water heaters: Ashland City TN, Johnson City TN, Lebanon TN, McBee SC, Juárez MX (3rd plant opened Sept 2024). [7]
- Distribution: El Paso, TX DC; Fergus, Ontario DC; Rancho Cucamonga, CA DC. [7]
- Water treatment: Appleton WI, Haltom City TX, Ontario CA, Pottstown PA (Groveport closing). [7]
