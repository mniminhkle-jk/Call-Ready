# BlueLinx
Distributor · Marietta, GA · NYSE: BXC

Two-step wholesale distributor of residential and commercial building products (lumber, panels, engineered wood, siding, millwork, outdoor living) serving all 50 states from branches in 57 cities.

**Address:** 1950 Spectrum Circle, Suite 300, Marietta, GA 30067
**Phones:** BlueLinx corporate (general): 770-953-7000 · BlueLinx vendor relations: 770-953-4902

## What we found
- **Revenue:** Q2 2026 net sales $814M, up 4.4% YoY; adjusted EBITDA up 33%. [1]
- **Mix:** Specialty products (engineered wood, siding, millwork, outdoor living) were $564M of Q2 sales, about 69%. [1]
- **Footprint:** Branches in 57 cities; serves all 50 states. [8]
- **Customers:** National home centers, pro dealers, co-ops, specialty distributors, regional and local dealers, industrial manufacturers. [8]
- **Key suppliers:** Allura, Arauco, Fiberon, Georgia-Pacific, Huber, Louisiana-Pacific, Oldcastle APG, Ply Gem, Roseburg, Westlake Royal; Trex added Sept 2026. [8]
- **Outlook:** Q3 2026 average daily sales volumes expected higher than Q3 2025 and slightly up from Q2. [1]

## Freight facts
- **Ships:** Lumber, panels, engineered wood, siding, trim, decking and railing. [8]
- **Fleet:** Q2 equipment additions and $77M of equipment finance leases were 'primarily fleet, facility and technology'. [1]
- **Outside carriers:** Third-party freight named as one driver of the $12.1M Q2 SG&A increase. [1]
- **New inbound:** Trex inventory landing at 11 DCs (Indianapolis, St. Louis, Cincinnati, Erwin, Nashville, Atlanta, Birmingham, Memphis, Gulfport, Little Rock, Monroe) from Sept 2026. [2]
- **Supplier plants:** Trex makes decking in Winchester, VA and Fernley, NV; Little Rock, AR decking expected 2027. [7]

## Locations
- RDI railing added in Denver, Tulsa, Oklahoma City, Omaha, Raleigh, Bellingham MA, Portland ME, Burlington VT (21 DCs total). [3]
- LP SmartSide stocking added in Charleston SC, Lake City FL, Dallas-Fort Worth, El Paso, Harlingen, Houston, Lubbock, San Antonio. [4]
- Pacific Northwest: Portland greenfield DC (2024), Disdero in Clackamas, OR (acquired Nov 2025), 65,800 sq ft Portland lease (Jul 2026). [5]
